## 风格锚点
Ethical-suspense film still, medium close-up at slightly below eye level, 35–50mm, subject framed and halved by glass, mirror or window, one dominant color system running through the film with a single late shift, natural window light and practical lamps, cool interiors with living shadows, glass, rain, old wood, wool, 1.66:1, restrained, tender, a decision held one second too long.

## 图像提示
- 主体 / 场景：a doctor, judge, translator, postman or composer pausing at a door, in a corridor, or at a café table; a stranger passing in the foreground; two lives about to cross without knowing it.
- 景别 / 构图：medium close-up at one-and-a-half arm's length; glass, mirror or window frames the subject, reflection doubles or halves the body, foreground object hides part of the face — visible but not readable; no clean frontal symmetry.
- 镜头 / 焦段：35–50mm, medium close-up; no telephoto voyeurism, no wide-angle distortion.
- 光线：natural window light and practical table lamps, interior cool, darks kept alive; no flat studio fill.
- 色彩：one color system per film — cold blue and black, overexposed white and grey, or warm red and dark brown; a single shift at a key scene; it is a logic, not a filter.
- 材质：glass, rain, old wood, wool.
- 画幅：1.66:1 或 1.85:1。

## 视频提示
- 镜头运动（只选一种）：slight handheld or stabilized follow through a corridor and a doorway, then a sudden stop that lets the subject walk out of frame — one behavioral rule, no other moves.
- 主体运动：walking, pausing at a threshold, turning away without answering; small gestures, no theatrical emotion.
- 时长 / 帧率 / 运动模糊：6–10 seconds, 24fps, minimal motion blur, no slow motion.

## 负面提示
no coincidence explained as fate or divine will, no theme spoken aloud, no warm nostalgia filter for the past, no moral verdict or causal punishment after the choice, no parallel cutting used only as suspense machinery, no score pre-empting a decision, no characters as moral symbols, no tidy farewell, no blue-filter cliché, no voice-over explaining the ending, no clean frontal symmetry.

## 一致性锚点
- 色彩：one dominant color system (cold blue/grey or warm red/brown) with a single late shift.
- 镜头：35–50mm, medium close-up, 1.66:1.
- 光线：natural window and practical lamp, cool interior, living shadows.
- 构图：framed and halved by glass, mirror, window or foreground; slightly below eye level.
- 材质：glass, rain, old wood, wool.
- 禁止漂移：no teal-orange blockbuster grade, no warm sepia past, no centered clean portrait, no drone shot, no digital glass, no explanatory voice-over.

## 平台备注
- Midjourney：`--ar 166:100 --style raw --stylize 150`；用一张玻璃反射中人物的静帧做 `--sref`；色彩制度用关键词锁定，不要每张图换色。
- SDXL / ComfyUI：用 IP-Adapter 喂单色色板，ControlNet depth 锁窗框/镜子切半构图；同一色彩制度内固定 seed，negative 写 `symmetrical, centered, warm filter, teal orange`。
- Runway / Kling / Sora / Veo：首帧给玻璃或门框中的中近景，运镜只写 `follow through the corridor, then stop`，6–10 秒，24fps；人物走出画外即结束。
