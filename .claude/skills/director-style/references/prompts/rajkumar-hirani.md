## 风格锚点
Warm Hindi commercial cinema, 2010s Mumbai, high-key soft window light bounced onto every face, clean ochre peacock-blue and cream palette, medium group shots with the whole ensemble inside one frame, eye-level 35–50mm, painted plaster and cotton shirts, fine film grain, comic timing held in stillness, a sincere face after the laugh.

## 图像提示
- 主体 / 场景：a scruffy outsider in a borrowed shirt standing at the edge of a hospital ward or classroom, five or six ordinary people around him, an authority figure seated dead center.
- 景别 / 构图：medium group shot, whole bodies and reactions visible; authority centered and symmetrical, trickster off-center; door or window used for eavesdropping, not oppression.
- 镜头 / 焦段：35–50mm at eye level, slight low angle; 85mm only for the confession close-up.
- 光线：high-key soft daylight through windows plus bounce; faces fully readable; one soft shadow side allowed only in the confession.
- 色彩：warm ochre, peacock blue, cream; medium-high saturation, clean, no neon.
- 材质：painted plaster, cotton kurta, steel tiffin, paper files, laminated hospital surfaces, fine 35mm grain.

## 视频提示
- 镜头运动：slow dolly in, one move only, toward the group, stopping before the face fills frame.
- 主体运动：the outsider talks with his hands, the group leans in, the authority straightens his back; small reactions, no slapstick.
- 时长 / 帧率 / 运动模糊：5–7 seconds, 24fps, natural motion blur, no speed ramp.

## 负面提示
no pure villain, no cruelty toward the poor or the sick, no handheld shake, no desaturated social realism, no close-up before the setup lands, no song as travelogue, no voice-over explaining motive, no neon, no cold green grade, no digital sharpness.

## 一致性锚点
- 色彩：ochre / peacock blue / cream, medium-high saturation, clean warm grade.
- 镜头：35–50mm, eye level.
- 光线：high-key window light plus bounce, readable faces, soft ratio.
- 构图：ensemble in one frame, authority centered, trickster at the edge.
- 材质：painted plaster, cotton, steel tiffin, paper, fine grain.
- 禁止漂移：no modern glass towers, no LED white, no desaturated grey, no shaky handheld, no digital skin.
