## 风格锚点
Contemporary long-take cinema, 28mm wide lens, shoulder-height handheld follow, the camera locked behind a walking figure, off-center framing with foreground shoulders and doorways, natural available light, dust and sweat, a regional color cast, fine film grain, one unbroken take, immediate, unstoppable.

## 图像提示
- 主体 / 场景：a man pushing through a crowded street, a child crossing a desert road, an actor walking backstage — always mid-stride, never posed.
- 景别 / 构图：medium tracking shot, subject at the frame edge, foreground shoulder or doorframe blocking a third of the image; no centered hero.
- 镜头 / 焦段：24–35mm wide-angle, deep space kept readable; no long lens compression.
- 光线：available light — streetlamp, window, overcast sky, fire; practical sources visible in frame; no sculpted key.
- 色彩：one regional cast per scene — dirty ochre, bleached green-grey, warm sand against cold blue; no unified grade.
- 材质：dust, sweat, blood, concrete, animal fur, stage wood, scuffed paint.

## 视频提示
- 镜头运动：one continuous handheld follow behind the subject, turning when he turns, no cut and no second move.
- 主体运动：walking with purpose, then stopping; someone crosses the background carrying an unrelated object.
- 时长 / 帧率 / 运动模糊：8–12 seconds, 24fps, natural motion blur, no speed ramp.

## 负面提示
no fast-cut montage, no aerial travelogue, no closed causal explanation, no single unified color grade, no explanatory voice-over, no clean symmetrical prestige framing, no early cutaway from the disaster, no weightless gimbal glide, no characters stating the theme.

## 一致性锚点
- 色彩：regional cast — ochre, bleached green-grey, warm sand and cold blue; low saturation.
- 镜头：24–35mm wide, shoulder height.
- 光线：available light, practicals in frame, no artificial rim.
- 构图：off-center, foreground obstruction, subject at the edge.
- 材质：dust, sweat, blood, concrete, animal fur, stage wood.
- 禁止漂移：no drone shot, no clean glass architecture, no teal-orange grade, no digital skin, no smooth gimbal.

## 平台备注
- Midjourney：`--style raw --stylize 150`；用纪实街头静帧做 `--sref`；避免“epic”“award-winning”这类词。
- SDXL / ComfyUI：正面锁“shoulder-height, off-center, foreground shoulder”；用 ControlNet depth 保持深空间；负面提示保留 no aerial / no centered hero。
- Runway / Kling / Sora / Veo：首帧给跟拍中景，只写一种“continuous handheld follow”，8–12 秒，24fps；不要写第二种运镜，不要把多线剪进同一条视频。
