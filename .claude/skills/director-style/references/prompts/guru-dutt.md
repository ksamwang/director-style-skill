## 风格锚点
Cinematic black-and-white film still, 1950s Bombay melodrama, hard side-and-back light slicing the face into lit and dark halves, 50–85mm compressed interior, subject pushed to the frame edge while an empty chair or bare stage holds the center, velvet, cigarette smoke, rain-wet street, 1.37:1, romantic self-destruction.

## 图像提示
- 主体 / 场景：a poet at a rain-streaked window with one half of his face lit; a singer at a microphone on an empty soundstage; a man in a wet street looking up at a lit window.
- 景别 / 构图：medium and close-up; subject at the frame edge, empty chair, empty stage or doorway occupying the center; slight tilt; mirror and window reflection used to double the face.
- 镜头 / 焦段：50–85mm, compressing the room so the figure has nowhere to retreat.
- 光线：hard side light and backlight, venetian-blind bars, mirror bounce; one half of the face lit, the other half lost; deep blacks, no fill.
- 色彩：black-and-white only, high contrast, tonal separation by light not by tint.
- 材质：velvet, heavy curtains, cigarette smoke, cut glass, wet asphalt, old soundstage rigging.
- 画幅：1.37:1 Academy.

## 视频提示
- 镜头运动（只选一种）：slow dolly in toward the face as the song rises, steady and smooth.
- 主体运动：restrained — a turn toward the window, a hand on the microphone stand, eyes held still while the body yields; singing without theatrical gesture.
- 时长 / 帧率 / 运动模糊：5–8 seconds, 24fps, no motion blur, no handheld, no speed ramp.

## 负面提示
no song-and-dance as decoration or music video, no song that resolves the conflict, no flat even lighting, no success-story ending, no blame placed on society alone, no woman as mere muse, no modern handheld realism, no self-pity turning into narcissism, no color, no clean digital sharpness, no symmetrical cheerful framing.

## 一致性锚点
- 色彩 / 镜头 / 光线 / 构图 / 材质：black-and-white high contrast, 50–85mm, 1.37:1; hard side-back light with split face and no fill; subject at edge, empty chair or stage at center, tilted frame; velvet, smoke, cut glass, wet street.
- 禁止漂移：no modern color grade, no LED even light, no handheld, no contemporary wardrobe, no bright high-key look, no soft-focus glamour without a dark half.

## 平台备注
- Midjourney：`--ar 1.37:1 --style raw --stylize 200`；黑白与高对比写在最前；`--sref` 用《诗人》或《纸花》静帧；加 `--no color`。
- SDXL / ComfyUI：chiaroscuro / film-noir 光照 LoRA（轻权重）；ControlNet depth 锁倾斜与人物压边；不要把 "beauty lighting" 写进 positive。
- Runway / Kling / Sora / Veo：首帧给半明半暗的脸，运动只写 `slow dolly in`；5–8 秒、24fps；歌曲段落单独生成，不要在同一段里叠加升降与横移。
